
---
#### Magnetic Field Strength (B)  $$B = \frac{\mu_0}{4\pi} \frac{2 M d}{(d^2 - l^2)^2}$$
$\mu_0$ - Permeability of free space
M - Magnetic Moment of dipole
d - Distance from the center of the magnet
l - length for $1/2$ of the magnet 

---
#### Magnet suspended horizontally in Earth's field $B_0$ oscillates with period $T$$$T = 2\pi \sqrt{\frac{I}{M B_0}}$$
T - Oscillation Period
I - Moment of inertia
M - Magnetic Moment of the dipole 
B - Magnetic Field Strength

---
#### pressure inside a bubble of radius $R$
  $$\Delta P = P_1 - P_2 = \frac{4T}{R} = \frac{8T}{d}$$
  T - Surface tension of the liquid film
  R - Radius of the soup bubble 
  $\Delta P$ - Pressure difference 
  
---
#### Poiseuille flow
$$\frac{dV}{dt} = -\frac{\pi r^4 P}{8 \eta l}$$
$\frac{dV}{dt}$ - Rate of change of volume with time
P - Pressure difference driving the flow between the ends
$\eta$ - Dynamic viscosity
r - internal radius of the capillary tube 
l - Length of the capillary tube

---
#### Upward surface tension force around circumference
$$F_{up} = 2\pi r T \cos\theta \approx 2\pi r T \quad (\text{for clean glass and pure water, } \theta \approx 0^\circ)$$
T - Surface Tension
