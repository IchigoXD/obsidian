# 🛸 PROJECT: ORBITAL HEART
### *A 5-Stage Cosmic Treasure Hunt & Birthday Investigation for Dile*

---

## 🌌 Mission Overview & Story Premise

> **Subject:** DILE  
> **Codename:** PROJECT ORBITAL HEART  
> **Origin:** Dimension 7-X  
> **Duration:** 5 Transmissions  
> **Status:** ACTIVE  
> **Threat Level:** 🟣 CLASSIFIED  

**The Premise:**  
Something has crossed dimensional boundaries and reached Earth. It wasn't supposed to. The entity knows Dile—it has been observing her for some time, but it cannot communicate directly. Instead, it has left a sequence of five classified transmissions scattered across her world. Each transmission reveals part of a larger cosmic message.

**The Twist:**  
*Someone from her own dimension is assisting the entity.* That person is **you**. She shouldn't know you are behind it until the final stages, as the mystery shifts from extraterrestrial anomaly to an deeply personal, heartfelt celebration of your shared history.

### 📡 STAGE 1 — THE FIRST CONTACT

* **Location:** Right in her immediate proximity (desk, study chair, bag pocket, or outside her room door) without her noticing you placed it.
* **Meta-Puzzle Fragment #1:** **O**

#### 1. The Alien Call
Call her using an alien / distorted voice filter. Speak slowly, calmly, and mysteriously:

> **"̷H̷e̷l̷l̷o̷... ̷D̷i̷l̷e̷..."**  
> *"Do not be afraid.*  
> *You have been selected.*  
> *Something has arrived in your dimension.*  
> *It left something for you.*  
> *Look around you.*  
> ***The object you are searching for is already within your orbit."***  
> *(Click. Hang up.)*

#### 2. The Classified Letter (Envelope #1)
Style the envelope like a classified extraterrestrial dossier:

> ### **TRANSMISSION 001**
> **ORIGIN:** UNKNOWN  
> **DIMENSION:** 7-X  
> **RECIPIENT:** DILE  
> **STATUS:** ACTIVE  
> 
> You are probably wondering why you are reading this.
> 
> Good.
> 
> Confusion means the transmission has reached the correct subject.
> 
> Something crossed the boundary between our dimensions.  
> It came looking for **you**.  
> We attempted to stop it.  
> We failed.
> 
> Before disappearing, it left five fragments of information.  
> The first fragment has already been delivered.  
> The second is waiting somewhere you visit almost every day:
> 
> **Find the place where knowledge is stored, but silence is expected.**  
> **Look for the coordinates hidden inside this transmission.**
> 
> Do not trust the obvious message.
> 
> **— X**
> 
> *P.S. You know someone who would never let you solve this alone. You just don't know who.*

#### 3. Puzzle & Clue Design
- **The Riddle:** Points toward the campus library / study hall ("where knowledge is stored, but silence is expected").
- **The Cipher:** Hide the exact shelf / row / desk coordinate using:
  - The first letters of each sentence, or
  - An alien substitution cipher, e.g.: `LQYF - 04 - 18 - 03` (matching specific words or book call numbers).
- **Meta-Letter:** Awards the first secret letter: **O**

> There is another human involved.  
> Her name is:

> Tell her the password.

#### 2. The Password Mechanic
- The password should be a personal word connected to you and Dile (e.g., an inside joke, a shared nickname, or an anagram hidden in Transmission 001).
- **The Exchange:**
  > **Yasini:** *"What's the password?"*  
  > **Dile:** *(states the decoded password)*  
  > **Yasini:** *(pauses, stares in mock disbelief)* *"Okay... so you actually figured it out. I was told not to give you anything unless you had the password."*  
  > **Dile:** *"So what do you give me?"*  
  > **Yasini:** *"Nothing. Check your envelope again."*

#### 3. The Secret Compartment
- The envelope Dile has been carrying holds a **false bottom** or an **unsealed hidden inner flap**.
- When unfolded, it reveals:

> ### **TRANSMISSION 003.5**
> You've been looking for clues across the world.  
> But you've forgotten something:
> 
> **The person who started this has been beside you from the beginning.**  
> Think about the person who:  
> • Knows your habits  
> • Knows the places you go  
> • Knows the things you say  
> • Knows what makes you laugh  
> • And knows exactly where you would never think to look.  
> 
> *Your next clue is closer than you think.*  
> — X

## 🔐 The Meta-Puzzle & Clue Personalization

To tie the 5 stages into a unified puzzle rather than isolated tasks, each stage rewards a letter for a 5-letter meta-word:

| Stage | Mission Fragment | Hidden Letter | Clue Association |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Stage 1** | The First Contact (Dossier & Call) | **O** | Something memorable you once told her |
| **Stage 2** | Campus Drop & UV Flashlight | **R** | A campus spot tied to a memory you share |
| **Stage 3** | Yasini & The Envelope Secret | **B** | An inside joke only you two understand |
| **Stage 4** | The Stealth Backpack Envelope | **I** | A nickname, song, or shared ritual |
| **Stage 5** | The Abandoned Car & Final Gift | **T** | The legendary abandoned car spot |

**Final Keyword:** **`O R B I T`** (Unlocks the tracker / confirms the mission completion).

## 📊 Puzzle Difficulty Progression

| Stage | Challenge | Difficulty | Key Sensation |
| :---: | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **1** | Phone call + hidden letter cipher | ⭐⭐ | Intrigue & bewilderment |
| **2** | Campus drop + human contact + UV search | ⭐⭐⭐ | Immersion & excitement |
| **3** | Password exchange + Yasini + double envelope | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ | *"Wait, how deep does this go?"* |
| **4** | Backpack plant reveal | ⭐⭐⭐ | *"OH MY GOD he planted this on me!"* |
| **5** | Abandoned car search + emotional reveal | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Pure payoff & emotional warmth |

> [!TIP] **Execution Rule:**
> Don't make the puzzles mathematically or cryptographically punishing. The thrill comes from **cinematic misdirection** and the gradual revelation that the entire world around her was in on the joke.
